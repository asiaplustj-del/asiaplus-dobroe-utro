Morning Wide: Today's Major News (November 25, 2026)

Jo Jaehaeng Seoul
Published 2026.11.25 06:06 View View Count
PIP 닫기
1. Seoul Subway Management and Union Reach Wage Deal, Call Off Strike

Negotiations between Seoul Metro management and the labor union were dramatically reached early this morning (November 25) right before a planned strike. As a result, Seoul Subway Lines 1 through 8 are operating normally as usual.

2. Both US Parties Clash Over Intervention in Jensen Huang's Return

As US President Trump stated that he directly intervened in Jensen Huang's return to Nvidia, even the ruling Republican Party has strongly protested. The Democratic Party also criticized that it raises concerns not only over national security but also corruption, and has signaled a high-intensity congressional investigation.

3. Russian Military Aircraft Turn Away Just Before Violating Airspace; Russia Claims "Planned Flight"

Yesterday morning, multiple Russian military aircraft entered the Korea Air Defense Identification Zone without permission and came close to violating airspace, prompting an emergency air raid alert to be issued in the Ulleungdo area. In response, the Russian Ministry of Defense claimed that its bombers and fighters conducted a planned flight over international waters and complied with international regulations.

4. "School Record Cheating" Results in Zero Across All Subjects, Up to Expulsion

As concerns over AI cheating grow, the Ministry of Education has decided to strengthen supervision of middle and high school internal exams to the level of the College Scholastic Ability Test (CSAT). It recommended that if cheating is detected, all tests taken on that day will receive a score of zero, with disciplinary measures up to maximum expulsion.
